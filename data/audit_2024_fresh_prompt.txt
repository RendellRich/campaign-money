I am auditing a pipeline that sorts political donors by industry, using what each donor wrote on their Federal Election Commission filing. I need your independent opinion on 100 records. Other raters are labeling the same records separately, and I will compare everyone's answers, so please label each one on your own judgment.

How to label

1. Label the industry of the organization the donor works for, not their job title. A lawyer at a bank is finance; a lawyer at a law firm is law.
2. If the donor is self-employed, left the employer blank, or wrote only a title as the employer (CEO, OWNER, PRESIDENT), label the industry of their own work from the occupation (a self-employed optometrist is health).
3. You may use what you know about a company, and look it up if you can (Jane Street is a trading firm, so finance).
4. Use unknown when the text gives no way to tell: a vague or unrecognized company name, a person's name, or a generic title with nothing else.
5. Confidence: high if most careful people would give the same answer; low if a reasonable person could pick a different one.
6. Use retired, not employed or missing only if the text itself says so (for example N/A or REQUESTED in both fields).
